Supplementary Videos for ELCOR-based Local Navigation

Di Tian, Dangjun Zhao, Haijin Zeng, Liang Hu, Yikang Bin, and Junchao Zhang

Abstract. This page presents supplementary of eight hardware videos for our ELCOR-based local navigation method in unknown environments under onboard sensing, localization, and control. In the real-world system, AMCL is used only for localization, the map is used only for localization assistance and visualization, and the planning pipeline uses only raw 2D LiDAR points throughout navigation. The proposed approach builds a visibility-aware linear convex obstacle representation from raw 2D LiDAR observations, preserving local boundary structure while maintaining a conservative form suitable for model predictive control.

Platform: a relatively large four-wheel differential-drive robot, 18 kg, 66.8 × 62.7 cm.

Experimental Note. AMCL localization contains noticeable errors in the real-world trials. Except for Trial 1, most obstacles are not well aligned with the map, and some obstacles were moved during Trials 6 and 7. The robot still safely passed through the environment and reached the goal.
